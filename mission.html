Mission Statement

Dear friends,

A mission statement is important is because it tells us the motivation of the church.

It tells us the driving direction of the church.

But I want to make this church democratic.

It has to be very well organized and specialized.

You see, there must be security.

When we talk about mission, we are talking about a goal, and when we set a goal, there are adversaries.

So, the structure of this government must be very intelligent.

Currently, it is just me.

But I believe that all people are trying to accomplish the right thing, and I want to align with what people want.

Since it is just me right now, I want to watch the news and provide my thoughts and commentaries on current events in my website.

I want to constantly state my mission and the goal that the society needs to go toward in every issue.

Maybe it is like a think-tank? I actually don't know what that is exactly.

I know I may be wrong, but I must let my opinions flow freely.

Thus, the website and church is currently run by just me, but I want to create a Baptist hierarchy.

This is not to say that I am the head of ekklesia - I am simply the head of this church that I created.

This is a church that I created, and other churches have their heads.

Thus, I want to coordinate with other churches and with other organizations to create goals and decisions and leads.

This website and church is something I will try to take a reigning stance on - to keep it to myself.

Whoever can take it, can take it.

But I believe the people of this world want me to keep it.

It will be a time to test the people and true democracy - will Dennis actually be the guy that all people vote for?

Will Dennis actually be representing all the people through this church as a pastor?

We shall test and see.

It may be a battle - all churches are a battle.

I read a book by Warren Wiersbe of 50 Christians we must remember.

There was a Christian who had a bleeding eye and he had to stop preaching occassionally because to dab the blood.

I know it's hard, but we must enter into the battle.

The mission of this church is to fix the problems of this world.

It is not to spread some fluff or joy - it is to remove the evil.

It is to wipe the blood

It is to bind the wounds.

It is to feed the poor.

It is to take what was stolen and give to the needy.

It is to correct injustice.

It is to fight for freedom.

It is to fight for God.

It is to defeat the works of the devil.

There are many good things we can do, but I think you see the point.

There is hurt and suffering everyday, and I don't want to turn my eyes away from it - I want to see it.

I want to see it, and and I want to feel it, and I want to heal it.

Until every human being and creation is praising God and rejoicing from no pain and has pure love only, the mission is incomplete.

May God be with us as we pursue this goal that is bound to be accomplished one day.
