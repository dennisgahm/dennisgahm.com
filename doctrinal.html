Doctrinal Statement

CFC believes that Christ is God, the Holy Spirit is God, and God the Father is the greater than Christ, as Christ himself said in the gospels.

The Bible is the guide for all truth. It is debatable whether it is inerrant or if it is completely understood correctly.

If you study the Old Testament books like Genesis, you find that Middle Eastern stories around the area had similar stories.

Therefore, it can be allgorical or a story that was combined from different sources.

However, my training (Dennis Gahm), was done under conservative leadership and Bible teachers, like John MacArthur in Grace Community Church

and I want to say that the Bible is accurate and trustable.

Where I want to diverge possibly is that I espouse General Revelation and find that all the religions in the world have something to contribute.

For example, the formal language and theory of Computer Science is scientific, verifiable, provable, and supposedly true in mathematical form.

We should use this body of academics for the glory of God and for our doctrine.

We believe that we should try to include everyone and that God made the world and the universe good.

A challenging question I faced in seminary is: what is evil? and what is good?

How did evil appear?

Regardless of this, it is our duty to try to make all things good and to have a positive mindset about everything.

We must give salt to things and say it is good when we have doubt, because we have hope for a good outcome.

But of course, we must be wise as a serpent and fight this evil that we all know is here in our lives and in this world.

And thus, our doctrine must constantly fight this evolving evil, and constantly evolve to purify and become the next level.

And is this not what the different councils in the Catholic Church do when they faced a certain heresy?

It is science that is the greatest enemy or simply greatest new event on our planet.

We see so many churches in dilapidated buildings saying hymns with no technology.

But don't we know that the Apostle Paul wrote in Greek because it was the newest thing and most popular thing at the time?

Now we need to write in 0s and 1s, and we need to understand what this binary means spiritually.

The doctrine of CFC is that of true theology, and because the name includes computers, we want to make computers involved in this church and theology and doctrine.

Thus, computer language and 0s and 1s is something we want to use to explain the spirit and theology and doctrine.

With love and joy, and the simple heart of the people of all time, even the simple primitive people, I want to continue the race with you

to find what it true and lovely.

And that is the doctrine we want to find - what is true and lovely - what is good.
