Eschatology

It is wrong for me to say, "I know the future."

But it is wrong for me to not give my prediction and interpretation - especially of the book of Revelation.

Jesus is reigning currently - the millenial and thousand year reign has started - a proposition I currently believe.

But why is it for a 1000 years? And is it a literal 1000 years?

I believe that with the technology we have with AI now, we will be able to live to the maximum life span.

This life-span may be 1000 years.

A Master's Seminary lecture told me that 1000 years was the original life span Adam was supposed to live - and all those life-spans in the Gnesis seem to be around 1000 years.

Thus, even with AI, it may be that 1000 years is all we live on this earth.

Thus, I believe it is a beautiful time in history - the millenial.

With Christ alive and reigning, everything is working for the good and there is only good humans alive.

Everything is getting better now.

In 3026, we shall revisit if the 1000 years is over.

I think it is possible for humans to live forever if AI is advancing rapidly enough.

It could be that hell and heaven are created in this universe.

But I believe that hell is spiritual and is a form of privation, rather than actual reality.

There is no need to fear hell, and when things are going fine, it's fine to say, "There is no hell."

We understand that all things for the good, and it will work for good for you too.

But there is a time after the millenial, when Jesus may disappear and die.

He may end his reign.

He is alive, but he just stops his help.

He wants to immediately let Satan come back so that they can fight him and end it quickly.

Therefore, during this millenial reign, we see everything getting better, and Satan or this evil still here, but we win every time.

But there is a time when we say, "I want perfection, I don't want this to go on forever."

That is when Satan is released, the millenial ends, and we fight Satan to destroy evil for good.

That is all I have for now.

Peace to everyone - no fear, and love
