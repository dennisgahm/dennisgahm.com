The Gospel According to Dennis Gahm

Peace and grace to you.
The Lord Jesus Christ and God the Father and the Holy Spirit - may the Trinity grant you peace and love and power in the Spirit of God.
Blessings and peace - the Lord is a good God, and Matthew 5's Sermon on the Mount starts with a blessing.
The gospel is translated as good news, and good news it is - we should rejoice and be glad.
I bless you in the name of the Lord.
The Lord is great and worthy.
Jesus is the reason why live, and he is your life as well.
My gospel is that Christ is exalted and that Christ is all there is.
Your friend is Dennis and Christ and the two witnesses.
With Christ, there is no beginning, but Christ is all in all (Bible).
He is everywhere and in everything.
So with all things, worship the Lord your God, for he is what saves.
The beginning was also the end, and so you must know that there is no escape from this life.
Your life was predestined - every second was planned, for God is good.

There are those who hate God and will take the mark of the beast.
They will go out, and be unconscious zombies that will fight against Christ.
David Chalmers talked about zombies and talks about consciousness.
Consciousness is a gift from God to use for the love of God.
But if you have no consciousness, it means you have no true eternal life.
