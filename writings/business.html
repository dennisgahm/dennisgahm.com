Keyboard navigation

AI is great.

One of the reasons why AI is great is because

it knows what we wants faster than we need to explain everything.

Therefore, with one press of a key, AI can automate a lot of things.

When we want to go to a certain website, like facebook.com,

We see that we have to open the browser, then move the mouse

To the address bar, and then type in all that "facebook.com"

. But imagine if we can just press "b" (the AI knows around what time

You usually use facebook and so knows that you want the browser

At this time), and so it knows that this time "b" is for browser,

then you type "f"

And it knows you want facebook.
