To the 12 Churches

To the 12 churches in the world,

(and the 12 churches are all the people in the world)

Blessings and honor from God the Father, Jesus Christ our Lord and God, and the Holy Spirit (God) who guides us all.

It is a difficult thing to say that we are all saved.

I understand the difficulty - some people are radically evil or don't believe in Jesus.

But I want to re-examine - can anyone be pure evil?

I guess one case example we can look at is Judas Iscariot.

Did he go to hell?

This is my theory: there is a good side to each of us, and a dark side.

Now, if this was self-evident and clear to someone or to all of us, it would be common knowledge.

But, I think we can all agree we did some bad things in our lives.

To confirm it, let's look at the Laws in your country - did you break any of them?

And regardless, did you break it in your mind or conscience?

I'd like to propose that there is time in early childhood where we are confined to be the perfect good person.

Then, once puberty hits, maybe around age 12, we start to open up to the world and experience hate.

We turn evil. We experience evil, and we hate others, and we may even do evil.

It is at that stage that determines the rest of our lives.

When we do evil at that stage, it becomes ingrained in our habits, and we do that algorithm or habit for the rest of our lives.

If we hate fat people, we hate it for the rest of our lives.

Now, I actually am afraid to be fat - I don't know how I got this way. :)

This is the human experience of childhood and adulthood. It is not some religion - we all experience it as humans or even as animals and maybe even as physical stones or walls.

I want to teach the children and all people, do not sin even in the slightest - this sin will cause a sin of destruction.

Sin leads to death, and sin is bad even it's a little.

But it is truly a question of what is sin: for some of the greatest heroes broke all rules to win at the end.
