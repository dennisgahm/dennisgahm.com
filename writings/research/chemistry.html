But all things fade.

Beauty is in the eye of the viewer.

And I say, "Thank God, help me."

We say we need him.

But we need God.

It is Christ we need, not some person or research.

And so we bow before the father on our knees, and give alms and worship to the Lord our God.

Thank you Lord, for the blessings and honor you have given us, and that we can give to you.

Father, forgive us for our sins, and forgive our debtors.

Help us to be more like the person you want us to be.

Help us to be like Dennis, and help Dennis to be more like all of our good side.

Help good people be good and bad people to get better and good.

Help the world turn good.

Destroy evil.

Evil is good some may say.

But I say, Lord help me!

Help me to worship you!
